● Common Symptoms

It never starts as an emergency.

It starts small — a dull ache you can almost ignore. Sensitivity to cold. A crack you can't see. By the time it hurts, the problem is already deeper than the surface. We treat all of it, same day:

Severe or sudden toothache
Facial or gum swelling
Chipped, cracked, or broken tooth
Knocked-out or loose tooth
Lost filling, crown, or bonding
Wisdom tooth pain
Abscess or oral infection
Emergency extractions & root canals

● Why It Hurts

Pain doesn't wait for a good time.

A cracked tooth, a lost filling, an ache that won't quit. Small problems rarely stay small. Painkillers buy you a few hours. They don't fix what's underneath.

Same-day care finds the actual cause and treats it, so you're not stuck guessing until Monday.

● Don't Wait
