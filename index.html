● Common Symptoms ●

Find What You're Feeling.

It starts small. A dull ache you can almost ignore. Sensitivity to cold. A crack you can't see. By the time it hurts, the problem is already deeper than the surface. We treat all of it, same day:

  • Urgent

    Severe toothache

    A deep, throbbing ache that keeps you up at night usually means the nerve is involved.

  • Urgent

    Swelling and facial abscess

    Facial swelling means infection is spreading beyond the tooth. This one cannot wait.

  • Urgent

    Dental infection

    Pressure, bad taste, or a pimple on the gum are all signs of an active infection.

  • Urgent

    Knocked-out tooth

    A tooth that has been fully knocked out can often be saved if you get here fast.

  • Urgent

    Loose or displaced tooth

    A tooth pushed out of position or moving in its socket needs to be stabilized quickly.

  • Broken or chipped tooth

    A cracked or broken tooth exposes the inner layers and gets worse with every bite.

  • Lost filling or crown

    An open tooth where a filling or crown used to be is exposed to bacteria and pressure.

  • Dental implant emergency

    A loose, painful, or failing implant needs a surgical eye on it, not a wait list.

  • Extreme sensitivity

    Sharp pain from cold, heat, or air points to exposed dentin or a hidden crack.

  • Wisdom teeth pain

    Pressure at the back of the jaw, swelling, or trouble opening your mouth.

  • Any other emergency

    Not sure what is wrong? You do not need a diagnosis to be seen.

● Emergency Dental FAQ ●

What Patients Ask Us Most.

+What are your hours?

Open Friday, Saturday, Sunday and Monday. Walk-ins welcome every open day. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are by appointment only. Friday 9:30am to 5pm, Saturday 8am to 10pm, Sunday 9am to 5pm, and Monday 6:30am to 5pm.

+What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe tooth pain, a knocked-out or loose tooth, a cracked or broken tooth, swelling in the face or gums, bleeding that won't stop, or a lost filling or crown that's causing pain. If it hurts or something broke, it's worth getting seen.

+Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome any day we're open, Friday through Monday. You can also book online or call ahead so we can have a room ready when you arrive. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are by appointment only.

+Can you treat a dental emergency the same day?

Yes. Same-day emergency care is what this practice is built for. Most patients are seen within about an hour.

+What should I do if my tooth gets knocked out?

Handle it by the crown, not the root. Gently rinse it, and if you can, place it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Then get to us right away. A knocked-out tooth has the best chance of being saved within the first hour.

+Is facial or gum swelling a dental emergency?

Yes. Swelling in the jaw, cheek, or neck can signal a spreading infection or abscess and should be treated quickly. Call us or come in right away.

+Should I go to the ER or a dentist for tooth pain?

For tooth pain, infection, or a broken tooth, an emergency dentist can treat the actual cause. An ER usually can't. If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, or trauma to the head, call 911 first.

+What if I can't pay for treatment right now?

Cost shouldn't keep you in pain. We offer 0% financing for all credit types, so you can get treated today and handle payment over time.
